# Sound Effects Attribution

This directory contains CC0 (Public Domain) sound effects sourced from:

## Sources:
1. **OpenGameArt.org** - "50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX" by rubberduck
   - License: CC0 (Public Domain)
   - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx

2. **OpenGameArt.org** - "512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)" by SubspaceAudio
   - License: CC0 (Public Domain)
   - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style

## To Complete Sound Integration:

1. Download the packs from the URLs above
2. Extract the zip files
3. Copy the following sounds to this directory:
   - `laser.wav` - Any laser shoot sound
   - `explosion_small.wav` - Small explosion
   - `explosion_medium.wav` - Medium explosion
   - `explosion_large.wav` - Large explosion
   - `impact.wav` - Hit/collision sound
   - `powerup.wav` - Power-up collection chime
   - `engine.wav` - Spaceship engine loop

No attribution required for CC0 licensed sounds.
